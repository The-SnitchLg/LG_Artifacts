## 3. Accessibility & Usability Guidelines (a11y)
<!-- TODO: Specify key accessibility rules to enforce. -->

- **Contrast Ratios**: Minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text.
- **Focus Rings**: Clearly visible focus indicators for all interactive elements.
- **Semantic HTML**: Proper heading order (`h1`-`h6`), ARIA labels for icon buttons, and form labels.
- **Touch & Tap Targets**: Minimum 44x44px target area on mobile viewports.
